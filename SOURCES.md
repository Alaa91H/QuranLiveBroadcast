# Background source note

The project now ships with a single local broadcast background at `web/assets/background.jpg`.

The exact clean background used in the design mockup could not be reliably identified as a public web asset. During the review, visually similar free-to-use Istanbul waterfront/mosque photographs were verified on Pexels and Unsplash, including:

- Pexels — “Sunset View of Istanbul Waterfront with Mosque” by Emre Simsek: https://www.pexels.com/photo/sunset-view-of-istanbul-waterfront-with-mosque-33229981/
- Pexels — “New Mosque at Sunset, Istanbul's Waterfront” by Adem Kömürcü: https://www.pexels.com/photo/new-mosque-at-sunset-istanbul-s-waterfront-37044497/
- Unsplash — “A view of a city from across the water” by Lesya Soboleva: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-view-of-a-city-from-across-the-water-cz8sn8G3MVQ

The bundled `background.jpg` is the clean background already present in the project, refined for broadcast clarity and used as the only photographic background so the application is not dependent on a live external image request.
